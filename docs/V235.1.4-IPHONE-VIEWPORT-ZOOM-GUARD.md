# ATM Town v235.1.4 — iPhone Viewport Zoom Guard

This hotfix prevents native Safari page zoom from being triggered by gameplay gestures.

- Keeps the existing `maximum-scale=1` / `user-scalable=no` viewport.
- Forces all iPhone Safari text-entry controls to 16px or larger to prevent focus auto-zoom.
- Marks the gameplay control surface and ACTION button `touch-action:none`.
- Cancels Safari `gesturestart`, `gesturechange`, and `gestureend` outside text entry.
- Cancels multi-touch page gestures during gameplay so joystick + jump/action cannot become a browser pinch.
- Text inputs remain focusable/selectable and keyboard behavior is preserved.

No gameplay, Money Rain, wallet, ATM Pay, multiplayer, or world-state behavior is otherwise changed.
