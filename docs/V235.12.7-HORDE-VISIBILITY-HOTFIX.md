# ATM Town v235.12.7 — Horde Visibility Hotfix

## Problem

The v235.12.6 Horde blackout was too dark and the local character could disappear. The blackout was rendered directly to the main game canvas and then used `destination-out` to carve the player circle. On the main canvas that operation erased already-rendered pixels, not just the darkness layer.

## Fix

- Horde darkness now uses a normal `source-over` radial overlay.
- The center of the player vision circle is transparent, so the player and nearby map remain fully visible.
- Vision radius is increased from the overly-tight v235.12.6 values.
- Maximum outside darkness remains strong at 0.92.
- Horde-forced night-map blending is capped below full night so it does not double-darken the scene underneath the blackout.
- Street-lamp lighting still renders after the darkness layer.

## Testing

1. Start The Horde outdoors.
2. Confirm the local player is clearly visible.
3. Confirm pavement / nearby objects / enemies are identifiable inside the player circle.
4. Confirm visibility falls off quickly outside the circle.
5. Confirm street-lamp pools remain visible away from the player.
6. Confirm distant unlit areas remain very dark.
7. Confirm downed/revive state is still visible within the local light circle.
