# ATM Town v232.1 Gameplay Hotfix

## Trade Beacon airborne anchoring
Trade Beacon overlays now use the same jump/jetpack lift value as the character sprite renderer. The selected XRPL NFT therefore remains above the local character while jumping or flying and also follows remote players while they are airborne.

## Fuzzy bot patrol
Fuzzy's previous patrol crossed authored blocked terrain in the streamed collision layer. His route was replaced with a loop around the arcade-side walkable area whose waypoints and connecting segments were validated against the current collision mask with stair overrides.

No XRPL transaction behavior changed in this hotfix.
