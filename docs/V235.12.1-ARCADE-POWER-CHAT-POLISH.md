# ATM Town v235.12.1 — Arcade + Power + Chat Polish

This is a polish-only follow-up to v235.12. It does not change Attribute Store payment networks, merchant wallet routing, Supabase commerce schema, or Testnet world-event reward policy.

## Neon Racer

- Mobile now uses a 560×820 portrait logical playfield.
- Desktop keeps the 900×520 landscape logical playfield.
- The canvas renders at its authored aspect ratio and is centered in the stage instead of being stretched independently on X/Y.
- Road edges, three lane centers, vehicle sizes, collision boxes, pickups, and spawn culling adapt to the active logical view.
- Orientation/resize rebuilds the backing canvas and repositions lane-bound objects without distorting sprites.
- The mobile in-game presence card is reduced so it does not dominate the road.

## Horde downed shadow

`drawPlayerSprite` now accepts a shadow-suppression flag. `drawHordePlayerSprite` passes that flag whenever the local or remote combat state is downed, so the sideways player no longer retains a standing foot shadow.

## Inferno

The Inferno flame renderer no longer exits when The Horde is inactive. The fire aura is visible in normal town/interior gameplay whenever the vending power is active. Zombie damage remains inside the Horde authority/combat update only.

## Invisibility

- Local user: faint self remains visible for movement/control feedback.
- Remote viewers: no player body, equipment, shadow, nameplate, weapon, jetpack flame, or other power visual is drawn.
- Remote invisible movement emits synchronized-looking footprints from interpolated multiplayer movement without adding another realtime message type.
- Horde targeting continues to ignore invisible players.

## Live Chat text size

A cog button in the Live Chat header opens a compact text-size panel with:

- Small
- Normal
- Large
- XL

The selected size changes message body, sender-name, and timestamp typography only. It persists in `localStorage` under `atm_live_chat_text_size_v1` and leaves the chat composer/HUD controls unchanged.

## Validation checklist

1. Open Neon Racer on a phone and confirm cars/road are no longer skinny or stretched.
2. Rotate the device and confirm Racer redraws cleanly.
3. Start The Horde, get downed, and confirm the normal standing foot shadow is gone.
4. Activate Inferno without starting The Horde and confirm flames render around the player.
5. With two clients online, activate Invisibility on one client:
   - invisible player sees a faint self;
   - other client sees no body/nameplate/shadow/weapon;
   - walking footprints remain visible;
   - Horde zombies do not target the invisible player.
6. Open Live Chat, tap the cog, change text size, close/reopen chat, and reload the page to confirm persistence.
