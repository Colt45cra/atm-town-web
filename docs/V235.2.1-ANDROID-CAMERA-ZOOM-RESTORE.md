# ATM Town v235.2.1 — Android Camera Zoom Restore

## Purpose
Restore intentional two-finger ATM Town camera zoom on Android without undoing the iPhone Safari viewport and multi-touch control protections added in v235.1.4/v235.1.5.

## Behavior
- Android/non-iOS touch devices: two-finger pinch on the game canvas changes the ATM Town camera zoom.
- iPhone/iPad/iPadOS: canvas camera pinch remains disabled so normal two-thumb gameplay cannot be mistaken for zoom.
- Native browser/page zoom remains blocked during gameplay.
- Joystick and jump/action pointer ownership remain independent.
- Desktop mouse-wheel/trackpad camera zoom remains unchanged.

No wallet, Money Rain, People Hub, multiplayer, voice, character, map, or Supabase behavior is changed by this hotfix.
