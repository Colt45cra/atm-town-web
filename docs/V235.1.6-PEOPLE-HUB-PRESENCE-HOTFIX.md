# ATM Town v235.1.6 — People Hub Presence Consistency

## Problem
The HUD online count used Supabase Realtime Presence while the People Hub Online page used only recent `player_state` broadcasts. A connected player could therefore be counted by the HUD but temporarily absent from the Online list.

## Fix
- Supabase Presence is now the authoritative roster for the People Hub Online page.
- `player_state` broadcasts enrich roster entries with live position, map, proximity, activity, character, and ATM Pay identity when available.
- Presence tracking now includes the public ATM Pay identity when available; no wallet address or secret data is included.
- Fresh broadcasts are retained briefly as a race-condition fallback until Presence sync arrives.
- `ATMGamePeople.snapshot()` reports the actual rendered roster length so the Hub count and list cannot intentionally diverge.

No SQL or schema changes are required.
