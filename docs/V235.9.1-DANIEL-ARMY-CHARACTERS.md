# ATM Town v235.9.1 — Daniel + ARMY Characters

## Summary
Adds the two supplied 768×1280, 3-column × 4-row sprite sheets as full starter playable characters without changing the synchronized Zombie Outbreak combat model from v235.9.

## New playable characters
- **Daniel** — `assets/characters/playable/daniel.webp`
- **ARMY** — `assets/characters/playable/army.webp`

Both use ATM Town's standard sprite layout:
- 3 columns: walk A / idle / walk B
- 4 rows: down / left / up / right
- 256×320 frames
- center column is the idle frame

The supplied transparent PNG sheets are converted to **lossless WebP** so pixel art and alpha edges remain intact.

## Integrated surfaces
Daniel and ARMY are available as starter characters in:
- Entry character picker
- Ready-to-enter/profile character picker
- In-world/local rendering
- Multiplayer remote-player rendering
- Locker character inventory
- Saved selected-character persistence
- People Hub avatars
- ATM Pay avatars

## Compatibility
This update is built on top of v235.9 Synchronized Zombie Combat and preserves its shared zombie authority, snapshots, fire effects, and world-event behavior.

No Supabase migration is required.
