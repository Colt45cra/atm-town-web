# ATM Town v235.6.1 — People Hub Mobile Scroll Hotfix

## Scope
ATM Town only. No Payload or Supabase schema changes are required.

## Fixes
- Gives the People Hub a definite viewport height on phones and installed PWA windows.
- Enables native vertical momentum scrolling inside each Online / People / Pay page.
- Keeps the game page itself locked while the People Hub page scrolls independently.
- Preserves each People Hub page's scroll position when live data rerenders.
- Stops the one-second online refresh from rebuilding the entire People Hub unless the visible online roster actually changed, preventing active touch scrolling from being interrupted/reset.
- Bumps the PWA shell cache so installed users receive the hotfix instead of continuing to use the previous cached People Hub JavaScript.

## Test
On a phone with at least three visible players, open People Hub → Online and swipe vertically through the complete roster. The list should remain at the user's scroll position while online presence refreshes in the background.
